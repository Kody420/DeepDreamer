Text to image · AttnGAN (2018)

  
A text-to-image GAN from the same era as the dreaming networks. It draws a 64×64 sketch, then refines it to 128 and 256 pixels,
    looking at the words of your sentence as it goes. birds only draws birds but does them well; coco tries everyday scenes and is very rough.
    Unknown words are ignored. Any result can become the start image of a dream video.
